Privacy Policy for Barakah – Faith Companion

Effective Date: February 16, 2026

At Barakah – Faith Companion, your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

1. Information We Access

Device Location: The app uses your device location to provide accurate prayer times and determine Qibla direction.

Local Notifications: The app may show notifications on your device for prayer reminders or daily tasks. All notifications are generated locally on your device and do not require an internet connection.

2. How We Use This Information

Location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction.

Notifications are used only locally to remind you of prayer times or daily tasks.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information or usage data.

3. Data Storage

All information used by the app stays on your device. We do not transmit any data to servers or third-party services.

4. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
